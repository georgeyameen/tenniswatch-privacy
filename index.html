TennisWatch Privacy Statement

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Hey there, Tennis Player!

I'm George Yameen, the creator of TennisWatch. I built this app because I love tennis and wanted a better way to track my swings. Here's the straight talk on how your data is handled.

What TennisWatch Does & Doesn't Do

TennisWatch uses your Apple Watch sensors to track your tennis swings and provide feedback. Your swing data stays on your device. We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app.

Your Data Stays Put

TennisWatch processes your swing tracking locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your swing stats, performance metrics, and training data stay on your devices. Anonymous usage data (screens viewed, button clicks, app interactions) is sent to analytics services to help us improve the app.

Analytics We Use

We use Google Analytics and Smartlook to understand how the app is used. These services collect: device type, iOS version, which screens you view, and how you interact with the app. No personal information or tennis performance data is collected. You can opt-out of analytics in the app Settings.

Kids Can Play Too

TennisWatch is safe for players of all ages. We don't collect any personal information that identifies users.
